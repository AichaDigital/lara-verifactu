## Critical data defects (emit invalid / fiscally-misleading XML)

### 1. `RegimeTypeEnum` is systematically misaligned with L8A/L8B and the XSD

Confirmed independently by the L8A, L8B and CROSS agents. The XSD type
`IdOperacionesTrascendenciaTributariaType` enumerates `01-11,14,15,17,18,19,20,21`.

- **`12` (SPECIAL_SIMPLIFIED) and `13` (SPECIAL_OBJECTIVE_ESTIMATION) DO NOT EXIST** in the XSD →
  emitting them = AEAT reject (error **1246**). Real simplified regime is **20**.
- **Systematic label shift 08-15**: `08`=agricultura (official: IPSI/IGIC), `09`=IPSI (official: travel
  agency mediators), `10`=billing-by-recipient (official: third-party collections),
  `11`=recargo equivalencia (official: business-premises lease), `14`=zona franca (official: pending-accrual
  AAPP works), `15`=no-sujeto (official: pending-accrual successive-tract).
- **Recargo de equivalencia confusion**: `SPECIAL_RECARGO_EQUIVALENCE='11'` is wrong — 11 is the lease regime.
  ⚠️ **Corrected after codex challenge:** `ClaveRegimen=18` ("Recargo de equivalencia", L8A:75) **IS a real, valid
  regime** (the retailer enrolled in the surcharge scheme). It is a *distinct concept* from the desglose surcharge
  fields (`TipoRecargoEquivalencia`/`CuotaRecargoEquivalencia`, which a general-regime issuer uses to *charge*
  surcharge to a customer — what AID-173 correctly implemented). The earlier claim "recargo is not a ClaveRegimen"
  was an overcorrection. `ClaveRegimen=18` (retailer scheme) is a scope decision (likely post-1.0), not a non-thing.
- **Missing codes**: 06, 17, 18, 19, 20, 21.
- **No L8A/L8B distinction**: single enum can't express IGIC codes 17 (comerciante minorista),
  18 (pequeño empresario), 19 (art. 25 Ley 19/1994) — **nuclear gap now that IGIC is core**.

### 2. `TaxTypeEnum` includes `IRPF='04'`

`04` is not in L1 nor the XSD `ImpuestoType` (`01,02,03,05`). A breakdown with Impuesto=04 produces XML
rejected by the XSD (error **1218**). Direct tax, meaningless in a Verifactu desglose. Remove or guard.

## Anulación / subsanación (L16/L17) — source-finding to respect

The L17 list (N/S/X) and `Subsanacion` belong to **RegistroAlta**, NOT RegistroAnulacion. The anulación
record uses `RechazoPrevio`→**L4 (S/N)** and a separate `SinRegistroPrevio` (L4). Rules 1153/1161 (RechazoPrevio×Subsanacion)
are alta-only. ✅ Implemented for core anulación (AID-182): `GeneradoPor` (E/D/T) + `Generador`
(NombreRazon+NIF), coupled per rule **1224**, NIF required (**1227/1228**, IDOtro post-1.0) and distinct from
ObligadoEmision (**1259**); `RechazoPrevio=S` ("anulación por rechazo"); `SinRegistroPrevio=S` ("anulación sin
registro previo"). `Subsanacion`/`RechazoPrevio=X` remain out of the anulación builder (alta-only).

## Codex challenge — corrections & new gaps (2026-06-16, 552k tokens)

Codex re-verified every strong claim against XSD/lists/errores.properties + code. **Confirmed**: 12/13 absent
from XSD, code 21, IRPF=04 invalid, RegimeTypeEnum 08-15 mislabels, S1 hardcode, E1 default, L16/L17 alta-only
finding (verified against XSD:109-110 alta vs :192-195 anulación), E7/E8, R2/R4/R5 labels.

**Corrections folded in above** (matrix was wrong/overstated):
- `ClaveRegimen=18` is a real regime (retailer scheme), not a non-thing — Critical #1 corrected.
- Builder does NOT "restrict to E1-E6"; it silently rewrites to E1 — corrected.
- "only 1284 implemented" → 1284 + structural 1118/1189 — corrected.
- "S2/N1/N2 silently mapped to S1" is imprecise: there's no `CalificacionOperacion` input to map; result is
  always S1 by *absence*, not mapping.

**Core gaps the matrix MISSED — resolved in AID-181:**
- **Macrodato** ✅: error **1139** — `Macrodato=S` emitted when |ImporteTotal| ≥ 100.000.000 (omitted below the
  threshold; minOccurs 0). AID-181.
- **F2 limit** ✅: error **1150** / §15.8 — F2 base+cuota capped at 3.000 EUR (+10 margin) via
  `assertF2WithinLimit`. The exception fields (NumRegistroAcuerdoFacturacion /
  FacturaSinIdentifDestinatarioArt61d) stay post-1.0, so the cap always applies to F2. §15.8 sums base+cuota
  only (the equivalence surcharge is excluded). AID-181.
- **Date validity** ✅: **1112** (FechaExpedicion not after the registration generation date) + **1152** (not
  before 28-10-2024) — `assertIssueDateValid`, compared as Y-m-d strings. AID-181.
- **`OperationTypeEnum` is a confusing public-API surface**: declares 01-07, maps to no official list, and
  `XmlBuilder` ignores `getOperationKey()` entirely. Remove or repurpose as the real `CalificacionOperacionEnum`.

**Scope tension (codex):** modeled regimes 08/10/20 *require* N2/N1/N2 (rules 1252/1205/1293). Since S2/N1/N2 are
post-1.0, core must hard-reject those regimes (the real régimen core is essentially `01` + domestic specials that
don't force N1/N2).

**Resolved (AID-183, against AEAT/BOE):** Veri*Factu (RD 1007/2023) applies to Canary issuers — the AEAT FAQ
(*ámbitos de aplicación*) states references to IVA are read as IGIC and Canary-domiciled taxpayers fall within
the SIF reglamento's subjective scope. So IGIC (`Impuesto=03`) is part of the Veri*Factu flow remitted to the
**AEAT**, exactly as the package emits it. The **SII del IGIC** (Canary libros-registro reporting to the
**Agencia Tributaria Canaria, ATC**, within 4 days) is a *separate* obligation — not Veri*Factu — and is out of
scope. Sources: AEAT FAQ ámbitos-aplicación, BOE-A-2023-24840, ATC SII-IGIC.

**Codex verdict:** mostly right on enum/XSD defects; was NOT mechanically issue-ready until the ClaveRegimen=18
fix, the overstatement corrections, and the 4 missed core gaps were folded in (done).
